Banana Pudding Dump Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 (3.4 ounce) packages instant vanilla pudding mix
  • 3 1/2 cups milk
  • 6 medium ripe-firm bananas, sliced, plus more for garnish
  • 35 vanilla wafers, plus more for garnish
  • 1 (9 ounce) package yellow cake mix (1 layer size)
  • 6 tablespoons butter, sliced into 12 pieces
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract

Directions

step1

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.

step2

Whisk pudding mix and milk together in a large bowl until thickened, 2 to 3 minutes. Gently mix sliced bananas into the thickened pudding; set aside.

step3

Arrange vanilla wafers in an even layer in the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Spread pudding and banana mixture evenly over wafers.

step4

Sprinkle cake mix evenly over pudding mixture. Arrange sliced butter evenly over the cake mix.

step5

Bake in the preheated oven until the top is golden brown and the filling is bubbly, 45 to 55 minutes. Remove from the oven and cool on a wire rack for 30 minutes. Refrigerate until chilled, at least 2 hours.

step6

Beat cream, sugar, and vanilla together in a medium bowl with an electric mixer until stiff peaks form.

step7

Spread whipped cream over the chilled cake. Garnish with additional crushed vanilla wafers and fresh sliced bananas before serving.
